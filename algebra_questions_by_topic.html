Algebra Questions by Topic

Topic 1: Express generalised numbers and express basic arithmetic processes algebraically 4 Questions

Question 1.1

Write an algebraic expression for the following situation:

  • A number is multiplied by 5, then 3 is added to the result.
  • Express this using the variable n.
Question 1.2

Express the following in algebraic form:

  • The product of two consecutive integers, where the first integer is x.
Question 1.3

Write an algebraic expression for:

  • Three times a number minus twice another number. Use variables a and b.
Question 1.4

Express the following process algebraically:

  • Start with a number, double it, subtract 7, then divide the result by 2. Use the variable y.

Topic 2: Demonstrate knowledge of substituting numbers for words and letters in formulae 4 Questions

Question 2.1

Given the formula A = l × w (area of a rectangle), find the value of A when l = 8 cm and w = 5 cm.

Question 2.2

If C = 5(F − 32)/9, find the value of C (Celsius) when F = 68 (Fahrenheit).

Question 2.3

Using the formula s = ut + ½at², find s when:

  • u = 10 m/s
  • t = 4 s
  • a = 2 m/s²
Question 2.4

Given V = πr²h (volume of a cylinder), find V when r = 3 cm and h = 10 cm. (Use π ≈ 3.14)

Topic 3: Formulate equations for given situations 4 Questions

Question 3.1

A book costs £x. A pen costs £2 less than the book. If the total cost of 3 books and 5 pens is £29, write an equation to represent this situation.

Question 3.2

The perimeter of a rectangle is 24 cm. If the length is l and the width is w, and the length is 2 cm more than the width, write an equation that relates l and w.

Question 3.3

Sarah is 3 years older than her brother. In 5 years, the sum of their ages will be 37. If her brother's current age is b, write an equation to find b.

Question 3.4

A car travelling at a constant speed covers a distance of d km in t hours. If the speed is 60 km/h and the distance is 180 km, write an equation relating d, t, and the speed.
