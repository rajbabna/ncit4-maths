📊 Probability Quiz

Multiple Choice and Short Answer Questions

Section 1: Single Event Probability (MCQ)
Question 1
A fair die is rolled once. What is the probability of rolling a number greater than 4? Express your answer as a fraction.
Question 2
A bag contains 5 red balls, 3 blue balls, and 2 green balls. If one ball is drawn at random, what is the probability of drawing a blue ball? Express as a decimal.
Question 3
In a deck of standard playing cards (52 cards), what is the probability of drawing an Ace? Give your answer as a fraction in simplest form.
Section 2: Combined Events Using Diagrams (MCQ)
Question 4
A coin is flipped and a die is rolled. Using a possibility diagram (grid), how many possible outcomes are there?
Question 5
A spinner has 3 equal sections (red, blue, yellow) and a coin has 2 sides (heads, tails). Using a possibility diagram, what is the probability of getting a red and heads?
Question 6
Two fair coins are flipped. Using a tree diagram approach, what is the probability of getting at least one tail?
Question 7
A bag contains 2 red balls and 3 blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. Using a tree diagram, what is the probability of drawing two red balls?
Section 3: Short Answer Questions
Question 8
A student rolls two fair dice. Draw a possibility diagram (grid) showing all possible outcomes. What is the probability of getting a sum of 7?
💡 Hint: Your possibility diagram should show all outcomes as points on a 6×6 grid.
Question 9
Draw a tree diagram to represent the following scenario: A person flips a coin and then spins a spinner with sections A, B, and C. List all possible outcomes and calculate the probability of getting heads and section B.
💡 Hint: Your tree diagram should show two levels of branching with probabilities labeled on each branch.
Question 10
Without replacement: A drawer contains 4 white socks and 2 black socks. Two socks are drawn randomly. Using a tree diagram, show the probability of drawing two white socks. Express your final answer as both a fraction and a decimal.
💡 Hint: Remember that the total number of socks changes after the first draw.

✅ Answer Key

Question 1: B) 2/6 or 1/3 (Numbers greater than 4 are 5 and 6)
Question 2: B) 0.30 (3 blue balls out of 10 total = 3/10 = 0.30)
Question 3: A) 1/13 (4 aces out of 52 cards = 4/52 = 1/13)
Question 4: C) 12 (2 outcomes for coin × 6 outcomes for die = 12 total outcomes)
Question 5: B) 1/6 (1 favorable outcome out of 6 total outcomes in the grid = 1/6)
Question 6: D) 3/4 (Outcomes: HH, HT, TH, TT. At least one tail: HT, TH, TT = 3/4)
Question 7: B) 1/10 (First red: 2/5, Second red: 1/4 → 2/5 × 1/4 = 2/20 = 1/10)
Question 8: Possibility diagram should show 36 outcomes. Pairs summing to 7: (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2), (6,1) = 6 favorable outcomes. Probability = 6/36 = 1/6
Question 9: Possible outcomes: HH, HA, HB, HC, TH, TA, TB, TC (8 total). P(Heads and B) = 1/6
Question 10: P(Two white) = 4/6 × 3/5 = 12/30 = 2/5 = 0.4
